Gryna means pure in Lithuanian – clean, genuine, free of anything unnecessary. That is exactly the approach to fashion we want to bring to every woman.

How it all began

GRYNA STUDIO was born from a simple belief: good taste should not be a luxury. In European cities, seaside towns and small cafés we were always inspired by women who look effortlessly elegant – light dresses, soft knits, comfortable shoes you can wear all day. We wanted that style to be easy to reach here too – without compromising on comfort, and at a fair price.

What we do

We select every piece ourselves and ask the same questions each time: will it be comfortable all day? Will it go with what you already have in your wardrobe? Will you still want to wear it next season? Only pieces that pass all three make it into the collection.

  • Timeless silhouettes instead of fast-fading trends.
  • Comfort first – soft fabrics, relaxed fits, comfortable footwear.
  • Fashion for every figure – many styles are available from XS to 5XL.
  • Fair prices and free shipping on every order.

Who we design for

Women who want to look good every day – at work, in the city, on holiday or at a celebration – and don't have time for endless searching. In our collections you will find dresses for every day and special occasions, blouses, knitwear, sets, shoes and accessories that are easy to combine.

Our promise

We want shopping with us to be simple and enjoyable. We reply to every email, are happy to help with sizing or styling, and always do our best to make sure you love what you buy.
