One-size items

Items marked “One size” have a relaxed or stretchy fit and usually suit sizes S–XL. Exact measurements, where known, are given in the product description.

How to measure

  • Bust: measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape horizontal.
  • Waist: measure around the narrowest part of your waist.
  • Hips: measure around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Foot: stand on a sheet of paper, trace around your foot and measure from the heel to the longest toe. If your feet differ, use the longer one.
  • Ring: wrap a piece of string or paper around your finger, mark where it overlaps and measure the length in millimetres.
