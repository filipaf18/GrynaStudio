Is shipping free?

Yes – shipping is free on all orders, with no minimum order value. Full details are in our Shipping policy.

Can I return an item?

Yes – you can return items within 30 days of receipt. The full return procedure is described in our Refund policy.

How can I track my order?

Once your order has been dispatched, we will email you a tracking number. You can also log in to your account to see all your orders.

Are payments secure?

Yes. All payments are processed by Shopify – a certified platform with SSL encryption. We never see or store your card details.

How do I choose the right size?

Our general size chart is on the Size guide page, and the measurements of a specific style, where known, are in the product description. If you are between two sizes, we recommend choosing the larger one.

Is there a discount on my first order?

Yes – subscribe to our newsletter and you will receive a 10% discount code for your first order. In addition, an extra discount is applied automatically in the cart when you buy 2, 3, or 4 or more items.

Do sold-out items come back?

Some collections are limited. If an item is out of stock, write to us – we will let you know if it comes back.

How can I contact you?

Email us at info@grynastudio.com – we reply within 24 hours on business days.
