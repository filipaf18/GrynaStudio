Can I return my order?

Yes. If you are not happy with the items you ordered, you can return them within 30 days of receipt. For a return to be accepted, the following conditions must be met:

  1. the item is returned with all its parts and components, and the packaging is in the same condition as when you received it;
  2. it shows only signs of normal trying on; damage caused by careless use is not accepted;
  3. items where hygiene matters must be properly cleaned before being returned;
  4. any defect is reported immediately after receipt;
  5. return shipping costs are paid by the buyer;
  6. we review the return within 7 business days of the items arriving at the warehouse – if the conditions are met, it is officially approved within this period.

We reserve the right to refuse returned items if they have clearly been used or damaged through no fault of ours.

How to return an item

  1. Write to us. Send an email to info@grynastudio.com with “Return request” in the subject line.
  2. Provide the details. Include your order number, the reason for the return, a photo of the item (if it is damaged), and your full name and address.
  3. Wait for the return address. We will send you the address of our central warehouse in Asia. Never send the item to the sender's address or any other address – only to the one we give you.
  4. Ship the item. Pack the item securely and send it with a carrier of your choice. Once it has been sent, email us the shipping receipt and tracking number – this helps us process your return faster.

Your responsibilities when returning an item

When returning an item to our warehouse in Asia, you:

  • choose the carrier and arrange the shipment yourself;
  • pay all return shipping costs, including any international shipping or customs clearance fees;
  • are responsible for packing the item properly and for making sure the parcel reaches the address we provide – we recommend a registered shipment with tracking;
  • send the parcel only to the address we give you – we cannot accept parcels sent to any other address.

An international parcel to Asia can take several weeks – we issue the refund once the item has reached the warehouse and been inspected, or once we receive proof that you have sent it.

These responsibilities do not apply to faulty or incorrect items – see “Faulty items” below.

Right of withdrawal (14 days)

If you are a consumer (a natural person entering into a contract for purposes unrelated to your business or profession), you have the right to withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days, without giving any reason, from the day on which you or a person designated by you received the last item of the order.

To exercise this right, inform us of your decision with a clear statement (e.g. by email to info@grynastudio.com) and return the items as described above. The direct cost of returning the items is borne by the buyer.

As orders are processed automatically and passed on for fulfilment immediately after being placed, we cannot stop an order that has already been dispatched. If the items are already on their way, please wait until you receive them and then return them – you can notify us of your withdrawal in advance.

Effects of withdrawal

We refund:

  • all payments received from you for the returned items, including standard delivery costs (if any were charged);
  • no later than 14 days after receiving your notice of withdrawal; we may withhold the refund until we have received the items back or you have provided proof of having sent them;
  • using the same payment method you used.

We do not refund:

  • return shipping costs;
  • additional delivery costs if you chose a more expensive delivery method than the standard one;
  • any loss in value of the item resulting from improper handling.

Faulty items

If you received a faulty or incorrect item, contact us as soon as possible at info@grynastudio.com and send photos of the item – the statutory quality guarantee applies.
