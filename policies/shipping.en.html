In short: shipping is free on all orders. Orders are prepared within 1–3 business days, and delivery takes 8–12 business days from dispatch.

Delivery times

StageTime
Order processing1–3 business days (may take longer if the order is placed on a Friday evening or at the weekend)
Delivery8–12 business days from dispatch

Shipping cost

We cover the shipping costs. All items are delivered completely free of charge, with no minimum order value.

Where items ship from

Items are shipped directly from our suppliers' international warehouses (including Asia). To offer fair prices, we consolidate shipments and send them in larger volumes, so delivery may take longer than buying from a local shop. Occasional delays are possible during busy periods – thank you for your understanding.

If your order is urgent, email us at info@grynastudio.com – we will be happy to help.

How to place an order

  1. Product. On the product page, choose the variant you want (size, colour) and click “Add to cart”. Check that you have selected the correct variants.
  2. Checkout. Click “Check out” and enter your correct delivery details.
  3. Review. Check the details you entered. Shipping is free on all orders.
  4. Payment method. Choose the payment method that suits you best.
  5. Confirmation. Complete your order – you will receive a confirmation by email. If you have any questions, please contact us.

Payment methods

All payments are processed in a secure, SSL-encrypted environment:

  • payment cards: Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, American Express;
  • Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay;
  • other methods shown at checkout.
